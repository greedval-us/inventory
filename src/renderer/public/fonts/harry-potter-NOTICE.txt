Harry Potter (Russian Version of Gfdtk), version 1.00, 22 March 2010.
Lettering based on Harry P by Phoenix Phonts & GeM. Cyrillic version by Gfdtk.
The upstream font includes its original font metadata.
The Inventory edition adds uppercase and lowercase Cyrillic Yo (U+0401, U+0451)
composed from the Cyrillic E and period glyphs of the same typeface.
